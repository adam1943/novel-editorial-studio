# Review Mode

## What to check
- Does the chapter/scene need to exist?
- What changes by the end: information, power, relationship, resource, risk, or decision?
- Is there a bridge from the previous chapter and a bridge to the next chapter?
- Does the authority jump make sense, or does it need an intermediate event?
- Does the relationship heat rise for a reason, or just because the outline says so?
- Is the text repeating the same function, summary, or setup?
- Does the prose smell like a prompt, a memo, or a writing manual?

## Common red flags
- sudden promotion or command without a bridge
- scene as explanation instead of scene as event
- repeated function across adjacent chapters
- generic emotion labels with no action
- inventory-style lists that replace drama
- template language such as “本章必须” or “状态变化与章末压力”

## Output shape
- Start with findings.
- Use a short issue table: severity, location, issue, evidence, minimal fix.
- Then list open questions or assumptions.
- End with a brief verdict or summary.
- If nothing is wrong, say that plainly and mention any residual risk.

## Outline repair preference
- `章节任务`: purpose only.
- `核心剧情简述`: direct story beats only.
- `审核意见`: logic, bridge, heat, repetition, AI smell.
- If a chapter reads like a prompt instead of a story, rewrite or flag it.
