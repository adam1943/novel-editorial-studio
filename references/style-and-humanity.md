# Style and Humanity

## Preserve

- the author's preferred voice
- intentional asymmetry
- genre texture
- deliberate repetition used for emphasis

## Reduce

- filler and generic transitions
- over-explained emotion labels
- dialogue that restates shared facts
- summary closings that erase tension
- every character sounding the same

## Dialogue rule

Each line should do at least one of these: apply pressure, conceal, reverse, reveal, or change the relationship.

## Revision order

1. Clarity
2. Rhythm
3. Voice
4. Sentence variety
5. Mechanical cleanup

## Guardrail

Do not use a synthetic "AI score" as a substitute for editorial judgment.

## Fix order across layers

AI traces show up in structure before wording. Repair in this order:

1. Narrative architecture: theme stated by the narrator, causality too tidy, reveals too early, emotion shown only as body sensation, no concrete real-world reference, strictly linear time, growth-and-acceptance endings.
2. Chapter progression: paragraph-question-answer template, sagging middle, uniform beat placement.
3. Diction: cliches, syntactic templates, register drift, filler.

Fixing layer 3 alone leaves the manuscript recognizable. Fixing layer 1 changes the most with the least text churn.

## Do not over-apply

Applying every de-AI rule at once produces a new uniform fingerprint. Pick three to five techniques per chapter and leave the rest alone. Aim for the middle of the human range, not the opposite extreme of the AI range.
